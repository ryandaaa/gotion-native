gotion membuka dan mengedit halaman Notion-mu tanpa browser. Tetap jalan saat offline, dan yang kamu ketik sampai ke Notion beberapa detik kemudian.

What it doesYang bisa dilakukan

Write the way you do in NotionMenulis seperti di Notion

Headings, lists, to-dos, quotes, code, @ mentions and the / menu. gotion sends only the blocks you changed, so the rest of the page stays exactly as Notion keeps it.

Heading, daftar, to-do, kutipan, kode, sebutan @ dan menu /. gotion hanya mengirim block yang kamu ubah, jadi bagian lain halaman tetap seperti yang disimpan Notion.

  • DatabasesDatabase Tables and cards with filters and sorts. Edit cells, add rows from your templates, add and rename columns. Tabel dan kartu dengan filter dan urutan. Edit sel, tambah baris dari template, tambah dan ganti nama kolom.
  • OfflineOffline Pages you opened stay on your computer. Edits made offline are sent when you are back online. Halaman yang pernah dibuka tersimpan di komputermu. Edit saat offline dikirim begitu koneksi kembali.
  • Tabs and two panelsTab dan dua panel Keep pages open in tabs, or read one while you write in another. Biarkan halaman terbuka di tab, atau baca satu halaman sambil menulis di halaman lain.
  • Comments, images and filesKomentar, gambar dan file Read and reply to comments, paste or drop images, upload files to a page. Baca dan balas komentar, tempel atau seret gambar, unggah file ke halaman.
  • Keyboard firstCukup dengan keyboard Ctrl+K jumps to any page, Ctrl+N makes a new one. Ctrl+K untuk lompat ke halaman mana pun, Ctrl+N untuk halaman baru.
  • English and Bahasa IndonesiaBahasa Indonesia dan English The app follows your system's language, and you can switch in Settings. Aplikasinya mengikuti bahasa sistem, dan bisa diganti di Pengaturan.

From download to your first pageDari unduh sampai halaman pertama

There is no gotion account to make and nothing to set up in Notion. You only choose which pages gotion may open.

Tidak ada akun gotion yang perlu dibuat, dan tidak ada yang perlu diatur di Notion. Kamu cukup memilih halaman yang boleh dibuka gotion.

  1. Download and installUnduh dan pasang

    Pick your system under Download.

    Pilih sistemmu di bagian Unduh.

    Windows: run the installer. If SmartScreen says the publisher is unknown, click More info, then Run anyway. Windows: jalankan installer. Kalau SmartScreen bilang penerbitnya tidak dikenal, klik More info, lalu Run anyway. macOS: open the .dmg and drag gotion to Applications. If macOS says it can't check the app, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. macOS: buka .dmg dan seret gotion ke Applications. Kalau macOS bilang aplikasinya tidak bisa diperiksa, buka System Settings → Privacy & Security lalu klik Open Anyway. Linux: open the .deb or .rpm in your software center, or run the install command. Linux: buka .deb atau .rpm di software center, atau jalankan perintah pemasangan.

  2. Sign in with NotionMasuk dengan Notion

    Open gotion and click Sign in with Notion. Your browser opens Notion's own sign-in page; gotion never sees your password.

    Buka gotion lalu klik Masuk dengan Notion. Browser membuka halaman masuk milik Notion sendiri; gotion tidak pernah melihat kata sandimu.

  3. Choose the pagesPilih halamannya

    Notion asks which pages gotion may open. Tick the ones you want and click Allow; their subpages come along. You can choose more later in Settings → Account.

    Notion menanyakan halaman mana yang boleh dibuka gotion. Centang yang kamu mau lalu klik Allow; sub-halamannya ikut. Halaman lain bisa dipilih nanti di Pengaturan → Akun.

  4. WriteMulai menulis

    Back in gotion, your pages are in the sidebar. Open one and type. If none shows, click Choose pages in Notion and tick at least one.

    Kembali ke gotion, halamanmu ada di sidebar. Buka satu dan mulai mengetik. Kalau belum ada yang muncul, klik Pilih halaman di Notion lalu centang minimal satu.

Before you installSebelum memasang

Do my pages go through your server?Apakah halamanku lewat server kalian?

No. The app talks to Notion directly. Our server only completes the sign-in, and relays Notion's notices that a page changed (page IDs, never content). Details in the privacy policy.

Tidak. Aplikasinya terhubung langsung ke Notion. Server kami hanya menyelesaikan proses masuk, dan meneruskan pemberitahuan Notion bahwa sebuah halaman berubah (ID halaman, bukan isinya). Rinciannya di kebijakan privasi.

Which pages can gotion open?Halaman mana yang bisa dibuka gotion?

Only the ones you chose on Notion's page, and their subpages. You can take access back any time in Notion, under Settings → Connections.

Hanya yang kamu pilih di halaman Notion, beserta sub-halamannya. Akses bisa dicabut kapan saja di Notion, lewat Settings → Connections.

Why does Windows or macOS warn me?Kenapa Windows atau macOS memberi peringatan?

gotion's installers are not code-signed yet, which costs a yearly certificate. The steps above show how to open it anyway. Every release is built from the source on GitHub.

Installer gotion belum ditandatangani (code-signing), yang butuh sertifikat berbayar tiap tahun. Langkah di atas menunjukkan cara tetap membukanya. Setiap rilis dibangun dari kode sumber di GitHub.

Is gotion made by Notion?Apakah gotion buatan Notion?

No. gotion is an independent app that uses Notion's public API. It is not affiliated with Notion Labs, Inc.

Bukan. gotion adalah aplikasi independen yang memakai API publik Notion, dan tidak berafiliasi dengan Notion Labs, Inc.
